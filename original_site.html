How coaching works

  1. We talk, you get clear

    A free 30-minute call. You tell me what's going on, and we decide if working together makes sense.

  2. Your first session

    One hour together. We turn a vague "something needs to change" into a goal you can measure.

  3. We set your rhythm

    After the first session, we decide together how many sessions a week suit you. Every session is one hour and ends with small actions you choose yourself.

My journey was never a straight line

Today I see that was the point. For years I thought living in the moment meant I was behind. It turned out to be the beginning of everything.

In 2018, the Miss India stage taught me how to hold myself in rooms I had never been in before. Then I moved to Canada for a post-graduate degree: a new country, a new chapter, and so much room to grow. If you've ever found yourself in an unexpected space while doing all the "right" things, I understand, and I know it can become the start of something new.

I earned my own way while I was there, and standing on my own feet taught me things no classroom could. Social work then brought me close to people living very different realities, and meeting people from so many places changed how I see people and life.

I don't have it all figured out. But I've lived enough versions of uncertainty to know how to sit with someone else's.

Not becoming someone else. Becoming more aware of who I am, and having the courage to keep evolving.Tanishqa
